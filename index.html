17 October 2026 · Your orientation day

For the girl beginning something new.

I’m so proud of you, Ma.

My Love,

Happy orientation day.

After the practical shifts, the reports, the studying, and everything it took to reach this point, here you are—beginning another part of your life.

I hope you let yourself feel proud for a moment.

I certainly am.

I hope this new chapter brings you kind people, opportunities to learn, growing confidence, and little moments when you realise how far you’ve come.

Give yourself time to find your feet, Ma. You’re allowed to ask questions and learn as you go.

And while you’re learning this new part of your life, I want to keep learning how to support you well.

I’ll do my best to listen when you need to talk, understand when you need rest, and keep making time for us with you.

Our days may become busier. Some calls might be shorter. We might have to plan a little more to catch each other.

We’ll find our rhythm.

A quick message before work. A little conversation when our schedules meet. A longer call when we have the time. Those moments will mean so much to me.

And we can keep telling each other what we need along the way.

I’m excited for your future, My Love. I want to hear about the things you learn, the people you meet, and the small victories you might otherwise forget to celebrate.

For today, take this new beginning one step at a time.

There is a very proud boyfriend behind this letter, cheering for you with his whole heart.

I love you, Ma.

Your Asvineee

Where Our Hours Meet

An original poem for you, Ma.

We once turned a little walk
into three hours of stories.
I think of that now—
how easily time became ours.

Soon, our days will have new shapes:
new places,
unfamiliar names,
little victories we haven’t told each other yet.

I want to hear about yours.

And when our hours don’t quite meet,
we’ll keep making room:
a message left for later,
a call we plan,
a sleepy kiss through the phone.

Go meet the days you’ve worked for, Ma.
I’m cheering for all you’re becoming.

Bring a hundred stories,
or sit with me without one.

I’ll be glad for the same reason:

it’s you.

The time we make for each other.
